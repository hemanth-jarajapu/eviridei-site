Eviridei Privacy Policy

Small steps, every day.

Effective date: 23 October 2026

The short version: Eviridei does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. Your tasks, notes, completion history, and settings stay on your device. Backups are created only when you ask for one, and they are encrypted on your device with a passphrase you choose.

This policy applies to the Eviridei Android application (com.passion.eviridei.yo), published by Hemanth Jarajapu (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

What Eviridei stores

Eviridei stores the information you enter in the app: task titles, notes, dates, times, recurrence rules, reminders, completion history, and display settings. This information is kept in the app's private storage on your device.

Eviridei has no account system and no server. We do not receive your task data, and we cannot read it.

Backups

You can export a backup from Settings. The backup is created on your device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your passphrase with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (210,000 rounds), and written to the single file or storage provider you choose. We never receive the file or the passphrase.

If you share a backup file with someone else, anyone who knows the passphrase can read it. Keep the file and passphrase somewhere safe. If you lose the passphrase, the backup cannot be restored. If you save the file to a third-party storage provider, that provider's terms and privacy policy apply to the file.

Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13 and newer): used to show task reminders.
  • Exact alarms (Android 12 and newer): used to deliver reminders at the time you choose. If you do not allow exact alarms, Eviridei can still use an inexact reminder window.
  • Receive boot completed: used to restore your reminders after the device restarts or the time or timezone changes.

Eviridei does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or broad storage access. It only accesses the single backup file you select.

No ads, analytics, or tracking

Eviridei contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting SDKs, and no tracking technologies. We do not use an advertising ID. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data to us or to a third party.

Third-party services

The app itself does not use third-party services that receive your data. This privacy policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web request information, such as your IP address, when you visit this page. That processing is governed by GitHub's privacy statement.

Your choices and data deletion

You can delete individual tasks and their history inside the app. You can also clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall Eviridei to remove the app's local data.

Backup files are not stored by us. If you created a backup, it remains in the location you chose until you delete it. There is no server-side account or data to request deletion for.

Children

Eviridei is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no account and no data collection.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this URL. Material changes will also be noted in the app's store listing or release notes where appropriate.

Contact

Questions about this policy or Eviridei's privacy practices? Open an issue at github.com/hemanth-jarajapu/Habtab/issues. We aim to respond within seven days.
